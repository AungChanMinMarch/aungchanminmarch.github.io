--- title: "9.1 Definitions" ---
  1. A nonempty set $X \subset \R^n$ is a vector space if $\vector{x} + \vector{y} \in X$ and $c \vector{x} \in X$ for all $\vector{x} \in X, \vector{y} \in X$ and for all scalars $c$.
  2. If $x_{1},..., x_k \in \R^n$ and $c_{1},..., c_k$ are scalars, the vector $$c_{1} \vector{x_1} + \cdots + c_k \vector{x_k}$$ is called a linear combination of $x_1, \cdots, x_k$. If $S \subset \R^n$ and if $E$ is the set of all linear combinations of elements of $S$, we say that $S$ spans $E$, or that $E$ is the span of $S$.
Show that every span in $\R^n$ is a vector space.
