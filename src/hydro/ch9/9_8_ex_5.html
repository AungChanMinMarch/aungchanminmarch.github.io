{{title}}Three parallel rectilinear vortices of the same strength $K$ and in the same sense meet any plane perpendicular to them in an equilateral triangle of side $a$. Show that the vortices all move round the same cylinder with uniform speed in time $\frac{2 \pi a^2}{3K}$.

Let $r$ be the radius of the circumcircle of the equilateral triangle $ABC$.

Let $O$ be the circumcentre.

\begin{align} \frac{OB}{BL} &= \sec {30 \deg} \\ \frac{r}{\frac{a}{2}} &= \sec {30 \deg} \\ r &= \frac{a}{2} \sec {30 \deg} \\ \end{align}

There are three vortices of strength $K(= \frac{\kappa}{2\pi})$ at $A$, $B$, $C$ which are situated at the points $z_{m} = r e^{\frac{2m\pi i}{3}}, m = 1, 2, 3$.

Then the complex potential of the vortices at $B$, $C$, $A$, is given by

\begin{align} w &= \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \sbracket{ \log(z-re^{{i120}}) + \log (z-re^{{i240}}) + \log (z-re^{{i360}}) }\\ &= \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \log \sbracket{ (z-re^{{i120}}) (z-re^{{i240}}) (z-re^{2 \pi i}) }\\ &= \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \log \sbracket{ (z^2 - rz e^{{i240}} -rze^{{i120}} + r^2)(z-r) }\\ &= \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \log \sbracket{ (z^2 - rz (e^{{i240}} + e^{{i120}}) + r^2)(z-r) }\\ &= \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \log \sbracket{ (z^2 - rz ({{e240}} + {{e120}}) + r^2)(z-r) }\\ &= \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \log \sbracket{ (z^2 - rz ( -\frac{1}{2} + i (-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}) -\frac{1}{2} + i (\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}) ) + r^2)(z-r) }\\ &= \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \log \sbracket{ (z^2 + rz + r^2)(z-r) }\\ &= \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \log (z^3 - rz^2 + rz^2 - r^2z + r^2z - r^3)\\ &= \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \log (z^3 - r^3)\\ \end{align}

Then the velocity induced at $z = r e^{{i360}} = r$, by others is given by

\begin{align} u_{1} - i v_{1} &= - \frac{d}{dz} \sbracket{ w - \frac{i \kappa}{2 \pi} \log (z - r) } \\ &= - \frac{d}{dz} \sbracket{ \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \log (z^3 - r^3) - \frac{i \kappa}{2 \pi} \log (z - r) } \\ &= - \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \frac{d}{dz} \sbracket{ \log (z^3 - r^3) - \log (z - r) } \\ &= - \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \frac{d}{dz} \sbracket{ \log \bracket{\frac{z^3 - r^3}{z-r}} } \\ &= - \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \frac{d}{dz} \log (z^2 + rz + r^2)\\ &= - \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \frac{1}{z^2 + rz + r^2} (2z+r)\\ &= - \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \frac{2z+r}{z^2 + rz + r^2} \end{align}

Thus the velocity induced at $z = r$, by others is

$$ u_1 - i v_1 = {- \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \frac{2z+r}{z^2 + rz + r^2} }_{z=r} = - \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \frac{3r}{3r^2} = - \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \frac{2z+r}{z^2 + rz + r^2} = - \frac{i \kappa}{2\pi} \frac{1}{r}$$

and its magnitude is

$$q_1 = \abs{u_1 - i v_1} = \frac{\kappa}{2\pi} \frac{1}{r} = \frac{K}{r}$$

Therefore the required time is

\begin{align} t &= \frac{\text{circumference of the circumcircle}}{\text{magnitude of velocity at $z = r$}} \\ &= \frac{2 \pi r}{\frac{K}{r}} \\ &= \frac{2 \pi r^2}{K} \\ &= \frac{2 \pi \bracket{ \frac{a}{\sqrt{3}} }^2}{K} \\ &= \frac{2 \pi a^2}{3K} \\ \end{align}
