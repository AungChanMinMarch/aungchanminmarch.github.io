--- title: "9.1D. Vortex tube and Vortex filament (or vortex)." ---

{{title}}

Vortex tube

The vortex lines drawn through each point of a closed curve enclose a tubular space in the fluid which is called the vortex tube.

Vortex filament

A vortex tube of infinitesimal cross section is known as a vortex filament or simply a vortex.
