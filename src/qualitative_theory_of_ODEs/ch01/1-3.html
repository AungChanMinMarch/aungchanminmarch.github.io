--- title: "1.3: Systems of first-order equations" ---

We shall study systems of fine order differential equations of the form

\begin{align} {y_1}' &= f_1 (t, y_1 ,y_2,..., y_n) \\ {y_2}' &= f_1 (t, y_1 ,y_2,..., y_n) \\ \vdots \\ {y_n}' &= f_1 (t, y_1 ,y_2,..., y_n) \\ \end{align} where $f_{1}, f_{2} ,...,f_n$ are $n$ given functions defined in some region $D$ of $(n+1)$ Euclidean space and $y_{1}, y_{2} ,...,y_n$ are the $n$ unknown functions. For a precise definition of region, see pg. 24. For the present, the intuitive notion is quite adequate. We shall see below that the systems considered in Sections 1.1 and 1.2 are special cases of the system (1.9). Το solve (1.9) means to find an interval $I$ on the axis and $n$ functions $\phi_1$,..., $\phi_n$ defined on $I$ such that
  1. $\phi_{1}'(t), \phi_{2}'(t), ..., \phi_n'(t)$ exist for each $t$ in $I$,
  2. the point $(t, \phi_{1}(t) ,..., \phi_n (t))$ remains in $D$ for each $t$ in $I$,
  3. $\phi_j' (t)=f_j(t, \phi_{1}(t), \phi_{2}(t) ,..., \phi_n (t))$ for each $t$ in $I$ ($j = 1 ,2,...,n$).
