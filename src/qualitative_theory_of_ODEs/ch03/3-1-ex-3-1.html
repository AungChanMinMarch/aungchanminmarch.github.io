Ex 3: Construct an equivalent integral equation to the initial value problem $$y'' + \mu^2 y = g(t,y), y(0) = y_0, y'(0) = z_0$$ assuming that $g$ is continuous in a region $D$ containing $(0, y_0)$ and where $\mu > 0$ is a constant.
$$y'' + \mu^2 y = g(t,y), y(0) = y_0, y'(0) = z_0, \mu > 0$$

Suppose $\phi$ is a solution of the initial value proble. The general solution is $y = y_c + y_p$. The homogeneous equation is

\begin{align} y'' + \mu^2 y &= 0 \\ \lambda^2 + \mu^2 &= 0 \\ \lambda^2 &= - \mu^2 \\ \lambda &= \pm i \mu \\ y_c &= A \cos (\mu t) + B \sin (\mu t)\\ y_c' &= - A \mu \sin (\mu t) + B \mu \cos (\mu t)\\ \end{align}

Since $y(0) = y_0$ and $y'(0) = z_0$,

\begin{align} A \cos 0 + B \sin 0 &= y_0 \\ A &= y_0 \\ - A \mu \sin 0 + B \mu \cos 0 &= z_0 \\ B \mu &= z_0 \\ B &= \frac{z_0}{\mu} \end{align} $$\therefore y_c = y_0 \cos (\mu t) + \frac{z_0}{\mu} \sin (\mu t)$$

Putting $y_c = \phi_1 + \phi_2$ gives

\begin{align} \phi_1 &= y_0 \cos (\mu t)\\ \phi_2 &= \frac{z_0}{\mu} \sin (\mu t) \\ \Phi &= \begin{bmatrix} \phi_1 & \phi_2 \\ \phi_1' & \phi_2' \end{bmatrix} \\ &= \begin{bmatrix} y_0 \cos (\mu t) & \frac{z_0}{\mu} \sin (\mu t)\\ -y_0 \mu \sin (\mu t) & z_0 \cos (\mu t)\\ \end{bmatrix}\\ \det \Phi &= \begin{vmatrix} y_0 \cos (\mu t) & \frac{z_0}{\mu} \sin (\mu t)\\ -y_0 \mu \sin (\mu t) & z_0 \cos (\mu t)\\ \end{vmatrix}\\ &= y_0 z_0 \cos^2 (\mu t) + y_0 z_0 \sin^2 (\mu t)\\ &= y_0 z_0 (\cos^2 (\mu t) + \sin^2 (\mu t))\\ &= y_0 z_0 \\ W&= \det \Phi = y_0 z_0 \end{align} \begin{align} y_p &= \int_{t_0}^{t} \frac{\phi_2(t)\phi_1(s) - \phi_1(t) \phi_2(s)}{W(s)} f(s) ds \\ &= \int_{t_0}^{t} \frac{ \frac{z_0}{\mu} \sin (\mu t) y_0 \cos (\mu s) - y_0 \cos (\mu t) \frac{z_0}{\mu} \sin (\mu s) }{y_0 z_0} g(s, y(s)) ds \\ &= \frac{1}{\mu} \int_{t_0}^{t} (\sin (\mu t) \cos(\mu s) - \cos (\mu t) \sin (\mu s)) g(s, y(s)) ds \\ &= \frac{1}{\mu} \int_{t_0}^{t} \sin(\mu t - \mu s)g(s, y(s)) ds \\ &= \frac{1}{\mu} \int_{t_0}^{t} \sin(\mu (t - s))g(s, y(s)) ds \\ \end{align} \begin{align} \phi(t) &= y_c + y_p \\ &= y_0 \cos (\mu t) + \frac{z_0}{\mu} \sin (\mu t) + \frac{1}{\mu} \int_{t_0}^{t} \sin(\mu (t - s))g(s, y(s)) ds \end{align}
