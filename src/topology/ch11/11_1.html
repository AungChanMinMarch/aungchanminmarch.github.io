Let $\classA = \{G_{i}\}$ be a class of subsets of $X$ such that $A \subset cup_{i} G_{i}$ for some $A$ subset $X$. Then $\classA$ is called a cover of $A$ and an open cover if each $G_{i}$ is open. Furthermore, if a finite subclass of $\classA$ is also a cover of A, i.e. if $$\exists G_{i_1} \dots G_{i_n} \in \classA \text{ such that } A \subset G_{i_1} \cup \dots \cup G_{i_n}$$ then $\classA$ is said to be reducible to a finite cover, or contains a finite subcover.
