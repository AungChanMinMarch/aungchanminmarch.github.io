Let $\base$ be a base for a normal $T_{1}$-space $X$. Show that for each $G_{i} \in \base$ and any point $p \in G_{i}$, there exists a member $G_{j} \in \base$ such that $p \in \closure{G_j} \subset G_{i}$.

Let $G_{i} \in \base$ and $p \in G_{i}$.

Since $X$ is a $T_{1}$-space, $\{p\}$ is closed.

Hece we have $\{p\} \subset G_{i}$ where $\{p\}$ is closed and $G_{i}$ is open.

By {% aTag collections.all "5208_10_4" "Theorem 10.6" %}, there exists an open set $G$ such that $\{p\} \subset G \subset \closure{G} \subset G_{i}$.

Since $p \in G$ is open, there exists $G_{j} \in \base$ such that $p \in G_{j} \subset G$.

So $p \in \closure{G_{j}} \subset \closure{G}$.

Since $\closure{G} \subset G_{i}$, we get $p \in \closure{G_{j}} \subset G_{j}$.
