The class $C(X,\R)$ of all real-valued continuous functions on a completely regular $T_{1}$-space $X$ separates points.

Let $a$ and $b$ be distinct points in $X$.

Since $X$ is a $T_{1}$-space, $\{b\}$ is a closed set.

Since $a$ and $b$ are distinct, $a \notin \{b\}$.

Since $X$ is completely regular, there exists a real-valued continuous function $f$ on X such that $f(a) = 0$ and $f[\{b\}] = \{1\}$.

Therefore. $f(a) \neq f(b)$.
