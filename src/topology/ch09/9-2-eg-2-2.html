Let $\topologyTau$ be the cofinite topology on $\R$. Show that $(\R, \topologyTau)$ is not Hausdorff.

Let $\topologyTau$ be the cofinite topology on $\R$.

Then $(\R, \topologyTau)$ is $T_1$ space.

Let $G$ and $H$ be non-empty $\topologyTau$-open set.

Then $G$ and $H$ are infinite since $\topologyTau$ is cofinite.

Hence $G^C$ and $H^C$ are finite.

Suppose $G \cap H = \emptyset$.

Then $G \subset H^C$.

Since $G$ is infinite and $H^C$ is finite, this is a contradiction.

$G \cap H \neq \emptyset$.

$G$ and $H$ are not disjoint.

Thus no pair of distinct points in $R$ belongs, respectively, to disjoint $\topologyTau$-open sets.

Thus $\topologyTau$ is not Hausdorff.
