Let $X$ be a $T_{1}$ space and let $\base_{p}$ be a local base at $p \in X$. Show that if $q \in X$ is distinct from $p$, then some member of $\base_{p}$ does not contain $q$.

Since $X$ is a $T_{1}$ space and $p \neq q$, there exists an open set $G \subset X$ such that $p \in G$ and $q \notin G$.

Since $\base_{p}$ is a local base at $p$ and $p$ is in an open set $G$, there exists $B \in \base_p$ such that $p \in B \subset G$.

Since $q \notin G$ and $B \subset G$, $q\notin B$.

Therefore $B$ is a member of $\base_{p}$ and $B$ does not contain $q$.
