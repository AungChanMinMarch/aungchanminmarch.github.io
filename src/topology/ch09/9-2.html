{{title}}

A topological space $X$ is a Hausdorff space or $T_2$-space iff it satisfies the following axiom:

$[T_2]$ Each pair of distinct points $a, b \in X$ belong respectively to disjoint open sets.

In other words, there exist open sets $G$ and $H$ such that $A \in G, b \in H$ and $G \cap H = \emptyset$

Observe that a Hausdorff space is always a $T_1$-space.

Theorem 10.3(Example 2.1)

Every metric space is a Hausdorff space.
