--- title: "Solved Probles no.1 (Theorem 10.1 proof)" ---

Prove Theorem 10.1

Prove a topological space $X$ is a $T_1$-space if and only if every singleton subset of $X$ is closed.

Suppose $X$ is a $T_1$-space.

Let $p \in X$.

We need to show that $\{p \}^C$ is open.

Let $x \in \{p\}^{\text{C}}$. Then $x \neq p$.

Since $x$ is $T_1$-space, there exists open set $G_x$ such that $x \in G_x, p \notin G_x$.

Since $p \notin G_x$, $G_x \subset \{p\}^C$.

Hence $x \in G_x \subset \{ p \}^C$ and $G_x$ is open.

$\{p\}^C = \cup \{G_x: x \in \{p\}^C \}$.

Since union of open sets is open, $\{ p \}^C$ is open.

Therefore $\{ p \}$ is closed.

Conversely, suppose that every singleton subset of $X$ is closed.

Let $a, b \in X$ with $a \neq b$.

Since $a \neq b$, $a \in \{b\}^C, b \notin \{b\}^C$.

Also, since $a \neq b$, $b \in \{a\}^C, a \notin \{a\}^C$.

Since every singleton subset of $X$ is closed, $\{a\}$ and $\{b\}$ are closed. Hence $\{a\}^C$ and $\{b\}^C$ are open.

Hence there exist open sets $\{a\}^C$ and $\{b\}^C$ such that $a \in \{b\}^C, b \notin \{b\}^C$ and $b \in \{a\}^C, a \notin \{a\}^C$.Therefoe $X$ is a $T_1$-space.
