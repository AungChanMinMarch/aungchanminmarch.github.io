{{title}}

A topological space $X$ is normal iff X satisfies the following axiom:

$[N]$ If $F_{1}$ and $F_{2}$ are disjoint closed subsets of $X$, then there exist disjoint open sets $G$ and $H$ such that $F_{1} \subset G$ and $F_{2} \subset H$.

Theorem 10.6: A topological space $X$ is normal if and only if for every closed set $F$ and open set $H$ containing $F$, there exists an open set $G$ such that $F \subset G \subset \closure{G} \subset H$.
A normal space $X$ which also satisfies the separation axiom $[T_1]$. i.e. a normal $T_{1}$ space, is called a $T_3$-space.
