Let ${{tau}}$ be the topology on the real line $\R$ generated by the open-closed intervals $(a, b]$. Show that $(\R, {{tau}})$ is Hausdorff.

Let $a,b \in \R$ with $a \ne b$, say $a < b$.

Choose $G=(a-1, a]$ and $H = (a, b]$.

Since ${{tau}}$ is generated by the open-closed intervals $(a, b]$, we have $G, H \in {{tau}}$

Clearly $a \in (a-1,a] =G$ and $b \in (a, b] = H$.

Also $G \cap H = (a-1, a] \cap (a, b] = \emptyset$.

Therefore $G, H \in {{tau}}$ such that $a \in G, b \in H$ and $G \cap H = \emptyset$. Hence $(X, {{tau}})$ is Hausdorff.
