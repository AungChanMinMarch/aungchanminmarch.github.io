--- title: "Section Formula" ---

Section Formula

To find the coordinates of a point $P(x,y)$ dividing a line segment $AB$ between two points$A(x_1, y_1)$ and $B(x_2,y_2)$ in the ratio $m:n$ , we use the section formula. $$x = \frac{m x_2 + nx_1}{m+n}, y= \frac{m y_2 + n y_1}{m+n}$$

Midpoint

To get the midpoint of $(x_1,y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$, we have to put $m=1$ and $n=1$ inside section formula. The midpoint is $\bracket{\dfrac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \dfrac{y_1 + y_2}{2}}$.
